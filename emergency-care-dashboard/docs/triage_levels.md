# Triage Levels

- **Level I (Red):** Life-threatening emergency requiring immediate attention.
- **Level II (Orange):** Emergency involving instability, risk, or very severe pain; attention is required within a very short time.
- **Level III (Yellow):** Urgent case requiring attention within 30–60 minutes.
- **Level IV (Green):** Minor emergency that does not compromise the patient's general condition; maximum wait of 120 minutes.
- **Level V (Blue or White):** Non-urgent case involving a low-complexity or administrative issue.

**Assignment notes:** Some records use Level VI; group these records with Level V. A small number use colour labels such as “yellow” or “orange”; the assignment suggests standardizing these to the corresponding triage levels.
